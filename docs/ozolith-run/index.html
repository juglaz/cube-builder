Avishkar · Ikoria · The Omenpaths

The race changed when the first crew came home.

When the Omenpaths opened, Avishkar's racing circuit spilled across the Multiverse. Pilots and artificers drove experimental Vehicles through unfamiliar worlds, building for impossible terrain and proving their machines under pressure.

The first run to Ikoria returned with fragments of The Ozolith. Near the crystal, modifications held and compounded: Hardened Scales stayed on the hull, Hangarback Walker kept growing, and salvaged parts carried traces of the machines they had belonged to. Crews started calling the largest remnant Ozolith, the Shattered Spire.

From that moment, the finish line was only half the race. The real prize was the next shard—and every crew wanted to reach it first.

The official face of the Run

Depala's Expedition

Depala, Pilot Exemplar organized Avishkar's strongest pilots, mechanics, and artificers into an expeditionary motor guild. Sram, Senior Edificer keeps the convoy legal. Pia Nalaar keeps it armed. Their crews chart the routes, breach the ruins, and bring the fragments home under guard.

They call their work recovery, not conquest. Every shard is catalogued. Every Vehicle is purpose-built: Peacewalker Colossus for the heavy runs, Cultivator's Caravan for the long hauls, Weatherlight and Valor's Flagship when the expedition needs a legend on the grid. Shorikai, Genesis Engine is the machine they do not take off the line.

But the distinction grows thinner with every victory. The more fragments Depala secures, the stronger her fleet becomes—and the more dangerous it would be to surrender them.

The race becomes a robbery

The Salvage Cartel

The Cartel does not waste time mapping ruins or surviving their traps. Its crews follow the people who do. Magda, the Hoardmaster counts the take. Reckless Crew makes the cut.

A convoy can spend weeks reaching a fragment and lose it in seconds on the return run. Raiders strike at speed, cut loose the cargo, and disappear through unofficial Omenpaths before Depala's heavier Vehicles can turn. None is more infamous than Smuggler's Copter. When the prize is too large for a copter, Magmatic Galleon and Hoard Hauler swallow the road whole.

When a Vehicle goes down, the Cartel turns the wreck into a second race. Engines, armor, cargo, and crystal belong to whoever reaches them first. What cannot be sold is stripped. What cannot be stripped is burned. Deadly Dispute and Costly Plunder are not metaphors. They are procedure.

Nothing useful stays dead

Greasefang's Gang

Greasefang, Okiba Boss came through a Kamigawan Omenpath with a simpler rule: a Vehicle is not dead because it hit the wreckfield. It is dead only when nobody can make it move. Okiba Reckoner Raid is how her gang introduces itself.

Her mechanics winch broken racers out of the dirt, hammer their engines awake, and throw them back onto the track before the dust settles. The repairs do not need to last. They need to hold through one more ambush, one more straightaway, or one final sprint. Carrion Cruiser, Demonic Junker, and Scrap Trawler are not pretty. They do not have to be.

Unlike the Cartel, Greasefang's crews do not see wreckage as inventory. They see unfinished races. A chassis abandoned by Depala or gutted by smugglers may return hours later under Greasefang's colors—faster, meaner, and carrying every old scar. Vat of Rebirth and Refurbish keep the dead in the race. Reckoner Bankbuster is the one they send when they want the finish line to remember them.

The fragments remember each other

The race is rebuilding the Ozolith.

Shards stored together begin to align. Distant machines develop the same unfamiliar modifications. Navigation instruments pull toward fragments no crew has found. Then one shard moves on its own—toward another. Animation Module and Walking Ballista do not stay the same size for long. Neither does the crystal.

The Ozolith did not simply break. Its pieces are trying to reconnect, and every faction has become part of the mechanism. Ozolith, the Shattered Spire is the wound. The rest of the cube is the scar tissue.

The outcome is never fixed

Every draft is another stretch of the Run.

Depala may secure the fragments aboard Heart of Kiran. The Cartel may lift them with Smuggler's Copter. Greasefang may put The Last Ride back on the track. The cards decide who wins this leg—and what the Ozolith becomes when the dust clears.
